Create your Slack bot

This opens Slack's app creator with everything pre-filled — events, scopes, Socket Mode. Takes about 2 minutes.

Step 2 of 3

Paste your Bot Key

Found in your Slack app under OAuth & Permissions.

  1. Open api.slack.com/apps → select your app
  2. Click OAuth & Permissions in the sidebar
  3. Look for Bot User OAuth Token — starts with xoxb-
  4. Click Copy and paste it above
Step 3 of 3

Two more codes

Almost done. Both are in your Slack app under Basic Information.

  1. Open your Slack app → Basic Information
  2. Scroll to App-Level Tokens
  3. Click Generate Token, add scope connections:write
  4. Copy the xapp- token and paste above
  1. Open your Slack app → Basic Information
  2. Scroll to App Credentials
  3. Click Show next to Signing Secret
  4. Copy the 32-character code and paste above

Fix 1 of 2

Add event subscriptions

Your bot needs these to receive messages. Paste the manifest into Slack and save — no URL required.

App Manifest · JSON (relevant section)
"event_subscriptions": { "bot_events": [ "message.im", "message.channels", "message.groups", "app_mention" ] }, "socket_mode_enabled": true
  • 1
    In your Slack app, click App Manifest in the sidebar
  • 2
    Switch to the JSON tab. Replace the entire contents with the full manifest
  • 3
    Click Save Changes
